When you start the MyWildin quiz, we ask for your birth date, and your birth time and city if you know them. People usually ask why, so here's the honest answer.

## The birthday part

From your birth details we read two old systems. One is Western astrology: your sun, moon, and rising sign. The rising sign needs a birth time, so if you don't know yours, that's fine. We just skip it. The other is BaZi, a Chinese system built on your birth date and hour. It gives you a "day master" and a main element.

We think of these as your factory settings. They're the defaults you showed up with, like the weather on the day you arrived. They can be interesting. They can give you words for things you already half-knew.

But they're not a verdict. They don't decide who you are or who you'll become. You don't have to live inside a label because of the day you were born.

## The part you write

The bigger part of MyWildin is you. You answer 15 short questions about real life: what you do when you're hurt, how you make decisions, how you keep friends close, how you handle change.

Those answers light up four layers:

- **Emotional:** how you feel things
- **Cognitive:** how you think things through
- **Social:** how you keep your people
- **Behavioral:** how you move when life shifts

Put together, they point to one of 15 animals. That animal is your Wildsign. It's a picture of your pattern right now, in your own words, not a box someone else put you in.

## What we don't do

We don't watch what you do. This version only knows what you tell it. Real behavior, what you actually do on an ordinary Tuesday, is something a quiz can't see, and we won't pretend it can.

Your birth date, time, and city also stay in your browser. When you share your Wildsign, the link carries the result, not your birthday.

## Why both?

Because they're different kinds of mirror. The sky part is fun and a little playful on purpose. Your answers are the part you actually choose. Seeing both side by side can start a good conversation with yourself: "Okay, that's my default. Is it still me?"

If you get stuck, or you're not sure what your Wildsign means for you, that's what Ask Amy is for. Amy isn't live yet, but she's coming.

Start with the factory settings. Then write the rest yourself.
