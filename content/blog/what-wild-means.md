"Wild" can sound like a party, or like a dare. That's not what we mean.

We use wild the way a field guide does. Animals in the wild aren't putting on a show. They're just being what they are: how they move, how they eat, who they stay close to. Your "wild" is the same idea. It's the real you, before you start editing yourself for other people.

## Why animals?

At the end of the quiz you get one of 15 animals. That's your Wildsign. It sums up your pattern: how you feel, how you think, how you keep people close, and how you move when life changes shape.

We picked animals because they're easy to get. "You're a Turtle" says more in a second than a chart full of percentages. You can picture it. You can laugh about it with a friend. You can say "okay, yeah, that's me" or "hm, not quite" and both are fine.

No animal is better than another. A Koala isn't a weaker Lion. An Owl isn't a quieter Wolf. Each one has its own strengths and its own blind spots. The Phoenix is the rare one, because the pattern behind it doesn't show up often. Rare doesn't mean better. It just means rare.

## Friendship first

MyWildin is about friendship. First with yourself, then with the people you actually want around.

This is not a dating app. There's no swiping and no chat. Dating is one kind of connection, and it may come later, but it isn't the point. The point is knowing yourself well enough to find your people and keep them.

A lot of us are lonely in ways that are hard to name. We have plenty of contacts and not many people who really get us. Knowing your own pattern is a good first step. It helps you see what you need from friends, and what you bring.

## So, what's your wild?

Not your loudest self. Not your best photo. Just the version of you that shows up when no one's grading. Take 15 questions and meet it.
