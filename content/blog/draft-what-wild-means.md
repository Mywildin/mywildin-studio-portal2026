MyWildin uses *wild* the way a field guide does. Not chaotic. Not a dare. The animal you get is a picture of a pattern: how you feel, how you think, how you keep people, how you move when life changes shape.

The reading is about friendship. With yourself first, and then with the people you actually want in the room. It is not a dating quiz wearing a moon.

An animal can be a clearer mirror than a percentile. The Phoenix stays rare because the pattern that calls it is uncommon.
